## 2. Contemporary Original Illustrations (Matri-Poshaka Jataka Storyboard)

All five narrative panels are modern vector artworks crafted specifically for this educational exhibition, inspired by the 5th-century Gupta-Vakataka murals of Ajanta:

1. **Scene 01: `assets/illustrations/scene-01-forest.svg`**
   - **Title:** *A quiet promise* (`01 / THE FOREST`)
   - **Classification:** Contemporary original interpretation.
   - **Description:** The Bodhisattva white elephant offering tender forest shoots and lotuses to his two blind elderly parents resting beneath an ancient bent Sal tree.
   - **Rights:** Educational project original, open for academic demonstration.

2. **Scene 02: `assets/illustrations/scene-02-stranger.svg`**
   - **Title:** *A stranger on the path* (`02 / AN ACT OF KINDNESS`)
   - **Classification:** Contemporary original interpretation.
   - **Description:** Compassionate encounter on a rugged mountain path; the elephant gently directing a fatigued traveller toward safety.
   - **Rights:** Educational project original, open for academic demonstration.

3. **Scene 03: `assets/illustrations/scene-03-betrayal.svg`**
   - **Title:** *Trust betrayed* (`03 / THE TURNING POINT`)
   - **Classification:** Contemporary original interpretation.
   - **Description:** Tense umber palette; the traveller pointing to the elephant's glade as royal attendants guide him away in cords; backward sorrowful gaze.
   - **Rights:** Educational project original, open for academic demonstration.

4. **Scene 04: `assets/illustrations/scene-04-palace.svg`**
   - **Title:** *A silent answer* (`04 / THE REALISATION`)
   - **Classification:** Contemporary original interpretation.
   - **Description:** Royal palace pavilion with untouched golden platters of sugarcane and sweet fruits; the fasting elephant silent in filial grief while the thoughtful King listens with deep perception.
   - **Rights:** Educational project original, open for academic demonstration.

5. **Scene 05: `assets/illustrations/scene-05-return.svg`**
   - **Title:** *The way home* (`05 / THE RETURN`)
   - **Classification:** Contemporary original interpretation.
   - **Description:** Warm dawn light beneath the same recurring bent Sal tree; joyous reunion and entwining of trunks between son and blind parents.
   - **Rights:** Educational project original, open for academic demonstration.

## 3. Textures, Typography & Libraries

- **Plaster Texture (`assets/textures/cave-plaster.webp`):** Procedural mineral grain texture crafted with multi-scale noise and muted ochre/umber tones, simulating aged lime plaster patina.
- **Typography:**
  - *Cormorant Garamond:* Designed by Christian Thalmann, licensed under SIL Open Font License (OFL 1.1). Loaded via Google Fonts with local system Georgia serif fallback.
  - *Inter:* Designed by Rasmus Andersson, licensed under SIL Open Font License (OFL 1.1). Loaded via Google Fonts with local Arial sans-serif fallback.
- **Animation Libraries (`assets/vendor/`):**
  - *GSAP (GreenSock Animation Platform) 3.12.5:* Standard GreenSock public license for web projects.
  - *ScrollTrigger 3.12.5:* Standard GreenSock public license.
  - Pinned locally for robust offline functionality with graceful progressive enhancement fallback.
- **Icons (`assets/icons/`):**
  - Clean outline SVGs based on Lucide Icons (ISC License).
