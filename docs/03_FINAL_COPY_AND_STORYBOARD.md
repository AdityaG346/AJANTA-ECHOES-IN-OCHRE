## Story intro
Eyebrow: **THE STORY**
H2: **The elephant who remembered kindness**
Intro: “A white elephant cares for his blind parents, helps a stranger, faces betrayal, and finds his way home. Follow the story through five painted moments.”

### Scene 01 — A quiet promise
Label: `01 / THE FOREST`
Caption: “In the deep forest, a white elephant cared for the two who had once cared for him.”
Body: “Away from the noise of the world, the elephant gathered food and stayed close to his blind parents. His strength was measured not by power, but by the care he gave.”
Visual: adult elephant with parents beneath recurring tree; quiet forest.

### Scene 02 — A stranger on the path
Label: `02 / AN ACT OF KINDNESS`
Caption: “When a stranger lost his way, compassion asked for no reward.”
Body: “The elephant found a traveller who could not find his way through the forest. Rather than turn away, he helped the stranger reach safety.”
Visual: winding path, lost traveller, elephant guiding him.

### Scene 03 — Trust betrayed
Label: `03 / THE TURNING POINT`
Caption: “The kindness he gave freely was repaid with betrayal.”
Body: “The traveller later revealed where the elephant lived. The forest that had felt safe now became a place of loss, as the elephant was taken away.”
Visual: betrayal and elephant led away, darker ochre/umber palette.

### Scene 04 — A silent answer
Label: `04 / THE REALISATION`
Caption: “No feast could comfort a heart separated from the ones it loved.”
Body: “In the royal setting, food and comfort could not ease the elephant’s sorrow. The king noticed that something was wrong and learned why the elephant would not eat.”
Visual: palace courtyard, untouched food, listening king.

### Scene 05 — The way home
Label: `05 / THE RETURN`
Caption: “The greatest gift was not a kingdom, but the freedom to return.”
Body: “Moved by the elephant’s devotion to his parents, the king allowed him to return. Back beneath the familiar forest tree, the elephant was reunited with the ones he had never stopped loving.”
Visual: return to original forest/tree, open warm framing.

## Reflection
Eyebrow: **THE STORY, TODAY**
H2: **An old story. A living lesson.**
Body: “Compassion is more than a feeling; it is a choice expressed through action. The elephant’s story asks us to consider how we treat those who depend on us, how we respond to betrayal, and whether we are willing to listen before judging.”
Three short values:
- **Care** — Strength can be expressed through patience and responsibility.
- **Gratitude** — Kindness should not be treated as something to exploit.
- **Listening** — Understanding another person’s story can change a decision.
Label: “Contemporary interpretation of the story’s themes.”
